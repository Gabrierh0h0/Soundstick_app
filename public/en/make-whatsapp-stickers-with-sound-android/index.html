SOUNDSTICK

How to make WhatsApp stickers with sound

A sticker with sound is a short image or video that plays audio when you tap it in the chat. Here is how to make one on Android, for free, with Soundstick.

Download for Android

What you need

  • An Android phone (7.0 or newer).
  • A short video, an image, or your own voice.
  • WhatsApp installed on that phone.

Step by step

  1. Download Soundstick and install it.
  2. Pick a mode: Video, Video + audio, Image + audio or Voice.
  3. Choose your file and move the box to decide which part becomes the sticker. Everything outside is transparent.
  4. Trim the video or audio with the timeline handles (10 seconds max).
  5. Tap Send to WhatsApp. The first time, link the app as a companion device: Settings → Linked devices → Link with phone number.
  6. The sticker arrives in your own chat, ready to forward.

Good to know

Soundstick is not made by WhatsApp or Meta. It uses an unofficial client, which may go against the terms of WhatsApp, and there is no guarantee an account will not be restricted. Use a secondary account. It only sends to your own chat.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. It is free and open source (GPL-3.0), with no ads or accounts.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet, Android only.
